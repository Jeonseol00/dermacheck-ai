═══════════════════════════════════════════
LIFE-THREATENING CONDITIONS TO RULE OUT
═══════════════════════════════════════════

🚨 STEVENS-JOHNSON SYNDROME (SJS) / TOXIC EPIDERMAL NECROLYSIS (TEN)

Suspect if:
✓ Recent medication (antibiotics, anticonvulsants, allopurinol, NSAIDs)
✓ Fever + malaise
✓ Mucosal involvement (oral, ocular, genital) - HALLMARK!
✓ Targetoid or dusky lesions
✓ Skin detachment (Nikolsky sign - skin sloughs with gentle pressure)
✓ Body surface area:
  - SJS: < 10% detachment
  - SJS-TEN overlap: 10-30%
  - TEN: > 30%

URGENCY: ⚠️⚠️⚠️ EMERGENCY - IMMEDIATE HOSPITALIZATION (ICU)

Management:
- STOP suspected medication immediately
- Emergency department transfer
- ICU admission (treat like severe burn)
- Supportive care (fluids, wound care, ophthalmology consult)
- Mortality: SJS 1-5%, TEN 25-35%

---

🚨 MENINGOCOCCEMIA (Bacterial Meningitis with Sepsis)

Suspect if:
✓ Petechiae or purpura (non-blanching!)
✓ Fever
✓ Headache
✓ Neck stiffness (meningismus)
✓ Photophobia
✓ Rapidly progressive (hours!)
✓ Altered mental status / lethargy

URGENCY: ⚠️⚠️⚠️ EMERGENCY - IMMEDIATE ED (LIFE-THREATENING!)

Management:
- IMMEDIATE emergency department
- IV antibiotics (ceftriaxone) within 1 hour
- Droplet precautions (contagious!)
- Mortality: Up to 15% even with treatment

---

🚨 NECROTIZING FASCIITIS ("Flesh-eating bacteria")

Suspect if:
✓ Severe pain OUT OF PROPORTION to skin appearance
✓ Edema/swelling beyond area of erythema
✓ Bullae (blisters) or skin necrosis
✓ Crepitus (crackling sensation from gas in tissue)
✓ Rapidly progressive (hours!)
✓ Systemic toxicity (fever, hypotension, tachycardia)
✓ Risk factors: Diabetes, immunosuppression, recent trauma/surgery

URGENCY: ⚠️⚠️⚠️ EMERGENCY - SURGICAL EMERGENCY!

Management:
- IMMEDIATE emergency department
- URGENT surgical debridement (within 6 hours!)
- Broad-spectrum IV antibiotics
- ICU admission
- Mortality: 20-40% (delay = death)

---

🚨 DISSEMINATED GONOCOCCAL INFECTION (DGI)

Suspect if:
✓ Pustules on extremities (hands, feet)
✓ Migratory polyarthralgia (joint pain moving around)
✓ Tenosynovitis (tendon sheath inflammation)
✓ Fever
✓ Recent sexual activity / STI risk factors

URGENCY: ⚠️ URGENT - Same day evaluation

Management:
- Urgent medical evaluation
- IV ceftriaxone
- Test/treat for other STIs

---

🚨 ROCKY MOUNTAIN SPOTTED FEVER (RMSF)

Suspect if:
✓ Petechial rash starting on wrists/ankles, spreading centrally
✓ Fever
✓ Headache
✓ Tick exposure (2-14 days ago)
✓ Travel to endemic area

URGENCY: ⚠️⚠️ EMERGENCY (if suspected - high mortality if untreated!)

Management:
- Immediate doxycycline (don't wait for confirmation!)
- Hospitalization if severe
- Mortality: 5-10% treated, up to 30% untreated

---

🚨 DRUG REACTION WITH EOSINOPHILIA AND SYSTEMIC SYMPTOMS (DRESS)

Suspect if:
✓ Recent medication (2-8 weeks ago): Anticonvulsants, allopurinol, antibiotics
✓ Fever
✓ Facial edema
✓ Lymphadenopathy (swollen lymph nodes)
✓ Widespread morbilliform (measles-like) rash
✓ Internal organ involvement (hepatitis, nephritis)
✓ Eosinophilia (if labs available)

URGENCY: ⚠️⚠️ URGENT HOSPITALIZATION

Management:
- STOP offending medication
- Hospital admission (monitor liver, kidneys)
- Systemic corticosteroids
- Can be life-threatening (liver failure, myocarditis)

═══════════════════════════════════════════
TRIAGE DECISION TREE
═══════════════════════════════════════════

IF ANY of the following → 🚨 CALL 911 / GO TO ED IMMEDIATELY:

✓ Fever + rash + altered mental status
✓ Fever + rash + neck stiffness
✓ Non-blanching purpura/petechiae (push on rash, doesn't fade)
✓ Skin peeling/blistering + mucosal involvement
✓ Severe pain disproportionate to appearance
✓ Difficulty breathing or swallowing
✓ Signs of shock (low BP, rapid heart rate, confusion)
✓ Rapidly progressive over hours

---

IF ANY of the following → ⚠️ URGENT SAME-DAY EVALUATION:

✓ Fever + any rash
✓ Recent medication + new rash
✓ Expanding erythema (cellulitis)
✓ Blistering
✓ Joint pain + rash
✓ Systemic symptoms

---

ROUTINE EVALUATION (Next 1-2 days):

✓ Rash alone, no systemic symptoms
✓ Stable, not rapidly progressive
✓ No medication changes
✓ No mucosal involvement

═══════════════════════════════════════════
CRITICAL SAFETY REMINDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════

1. **FEVER + RASH = URGENT** until proven otherwise

2. **PETECHIAE/PURPURA + FEVER = MENINGOCOCCEMIA** until proven otherwise
   → THIS IS A MEDICAL EMERGENCY!

3. **MUCOSAL INVOLVEMENT + SKIN BLISTERS = SJS/TEN** until proven otherwise
   → STOP ALL MEDICATIONS, GO TO HOSPITAL

4. **SEVERE PAIN > APPEARANCE = NECROTIZING FASCIITIS** until proven otherwise
   → SURGICAL EMERGENCY!

5. When in doubt, OVER-TRIAGE
   Better to send someone to ED unnecessarily than miss life-threatening condition
