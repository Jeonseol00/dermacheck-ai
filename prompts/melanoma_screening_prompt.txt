ABCDE CRITERIA EVALUATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════

A - ASYMMETRY:
If you draw a line through the middle, do the two halves match?
- Symmetric (reassuring)
- Asymmetric in 1 axis (concerning)
- Asymmetric in 2 axes (highly concerning)

Score: [0 = symmetric / 1 = asymmetric]

B - BORDER IRREGULARITY:
Are the edges smooth and even, or notched/scalloped/poorly defined?
- Smooth, well-defined (reassuring)
- Slightly irregular
- Markedly irregular, notched (concerning)

Score: [0 = smooth / 1 = irregular]

C - COLOR VARIATION:
Is the color uniform, or multiple shades present?
Single color (reassuring):
- Uniform tan, brown, or black

Multiple colors (concerning):
- Tan AND brown
- Brown AND black
- Red, white, or blue within lesion (highly concerning!)

Score: [0 = uniform / 1 = varied / 2 = multiple + red/white/blue]

D - DIAMETER:
What is the size of the lesion?
- < 6 mm (reassuring, but melanoma can be smaller!)
- ≥ 6 mm (concerning - size of pencil eraser)
- > 10 mm (more concern ing)

Score: [0 = <6mm / 1 = ≥6mm]

E - EVOLUTION (MOST IMPORTANT!):
Has the lesion changed over time?
- Stable for years (reassuring)
- Growing in size (concerning)
- Changing color (concerning)
- Changing shape (concerning)
- New symptoms: itch, bleed, crust (concerning)

"The ugly duckling" - Does it look different from other moles?

Score: [0 = stable / 1 = evolving]

═══════════════════════════════════════════
SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR SKIN OF COLOR
 ═══════════════════════════════════════════

⚠️ If Fitzpatrick IV-VI (Darker skin):

ACRAL LENTIGINOUS MELANOMA (ALM):
Most common melanoma subtype in people of color!
NOT related to sun exposure.

High-risk locations:
- Palms of hands
- Soles of feet
- Under nails (subungual)
- Mucous membranes (oral, genital)

RED FLAGS for ALM:
🚨 Pigmented lesion on palm/sole
🚨 Longitudinal pigmented band in nail (melanonychia)
🚨 Hutchinson's sign: Pigment extends from nail to surrounding skin (CRITICAL!)
🚨 Nail dystrophy or nail separation
🚨 Pigmented lesion in mouth or genital area

ALM characteristics:
- Often diagnosed late (poor outcomes!)
- Irregular borders
- Diameter often > 6mm at presentation
- Color may be uniform dark brown/black (vs variegated)
- MUST have low threshold for biopsy

If acral/mucosal location + pigmentation → URGENT dermatology referral!

═══════════════════════════════════════════
ADDITIONAL RISK FACTORS
═══════════════════════════════════════════

HIGH RISK (Refer for full skin exam):
- > 50 moles total
- Atypical/dysplastic nevi
- Large congenital nevi
- Previous melanoma or other skin cancer
- Family history of melanoma
- Immunosuppression (transplant, HIV)
- BRAF/CDKN2A gene mutations

ENVIRONMENTAL:
- Severe sunburns (especially childhood)
- Tanning bed use
- High cumulative sun exposure

═══════════════════════════════════════════
DERMOSCOPY CLUES (if available)
═══════════════════════════════════════════

Benign features:
- Uniform pigment network
- Central hypopigmentation
- Regular dots/globules

Concerning features:
- Atypical network (irregular holes)
- Blue-white veil
- Irregular streaks
- Multiple colors
- Regression areas
- Asymmetric pigmentation

⚠️ ZERO TOLERANCE FOR FALSE NEGATIVES
When in doubt, REFER!

Melanoma is life-threatening if not caught early.
5-year survival:
- Localized (early): >99%
- Regional spread: 66%
- Distant metastasis: 27%

Better to biopsy 10 benign moles than miss 1 melanoma.

Any concerning pigmented lesion requires dermatologist evaluation + biopsy for DEFINITIVE diagnosis.
